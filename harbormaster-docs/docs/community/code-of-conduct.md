# Code of conduct

This page states the standards for behavior in the Harbormaster community. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project's issues, discussions, pull requests and other spaces.

Harbormaster adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) by reference. The full text at that link is the code of conduct.

## Summary

We want a community that is open and welcoming, with a harassment-free experience for everyone.

Expected:

- Be respectful and kind.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through GitHub. Use private vulnerability reporting only for security issues. For conduct concerns, open a private conversation with a maintainer from their GitHub profile, or ask in an issue for a maintainer to contact you without sharing details publicly. Reports are handled with care for the privacy of the reporter.

Maintainers follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
